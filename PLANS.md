# Development roadmap and authorization

The phase sequence follows `docs/source-requirements.md` sections 29–36, refined by later owner decisions. Scope and acceptance descriptions are planning material. **Phase 1 was explicitly authorized by the owner on 2026-10-25** under the approximate monthly modeling profile. Phases 2–7 remain locked. See `harness/status.md` before acting.

| Phase | Scope and deliverables | Dependency and acceptance evidence | Risks and explicit exclusions | Authorization |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 — Repository foundation and Codex configuration | Python project setup, justified Codex configuration, infrastructure checks, CI, package entry point, minimal GUI smoke shell, documentation, and phase plans. | Installation, import, startup, tests, lint, types, configuration, and preservation checks; see `harness/build/phase-00-foundation.md` and `harness/context/phase-00-foundation.md`. | Premature dependencies, unsupported hooks, and false claims of active safeguards. Excludes product financial features. | **Local criteria and hosted three-system CI gate met for published commit `3642027` (run `41320491886`).** |
| 1 — Financial calculation engine | Approximate equal-monthly-period models, derived values, repayment schedules, fixed/periodically fixed/variable rate paths, selected overpayments, fees, cost totals, and simplified effective annual interest. | Independent formula, regression, property, edge-case, and cost-allocation checks passed locally; see `harness/context/phase-01-financial-engine.md`. | Approximate results may differ from bank schedules. Excludes actual-day accrual, full RRSO, GUI, persistence, exports, and providers. | **Core criteria and hosted three-system CI gate met for published commit `3642027` (run `41320491886`); Phase 2 remains locked.** |
| 2 — Desktop interface and persistence | PySide6 simulation workflow, local SQLite history, and basic single-scenario charts. | Depends on verified engine and approved storage/UI decisions. GUI interaction, migration, startup, and offline tests are required. | Data loss, blocking UI, invalid input. Excludes comparison and optimization. | **LOCKED** |
| 3 — Comparison and visualization | Multi-scenario comparisons, charts, cost waterfall, and sensitivity views. | Depends on reproducible scenarios. Chart-to-engine reconciliation and comparison regression tests are required. | Misleading comparisons under different assumptions. Excludes optimization ranking. | **LOCKED** |
| 4 — Risk and optimization | Transparent cost and safety criteria, stress tests, break-even analysis, constrained search, and Pareto views. | Depends on verified calculation and comparison. Objective, infeasibility, stress, and independent correctness checks are required. | False certainty, hidden scoring, combinatorial growth. Excludes guaranteed recommendations. | **LOCKED** |
| 5 — Reporting and exports | PDF and XLSX reports with calculations, assumptions, provenance, and limitations. | Depends on verified results. Parse/open files, verify structure and contents, and reconcile totals. | Formatting and numeric discrepancies. Excludes external data acquisition. | **LOCKED** |
| 6 — Historical data and providers | CSV import, verified rate providers, provenance, missing-data rules, and backtesting. | Depends on explicit index definitions and lawful sources. Mocked, offline, provenance, and reproducibility tests are required. | Licensing, unavailable observations, false historical reconstruction. Excludes fabricated data. | **LOCKED** |
| 7 — Advanced features and distribution | Candidates: refinancing, present value, Monte Carlo, localization, and installers. Exact scope requires a new decision. | Depends on prior phases and an approved detailed specification. Tests depend on selected features and target platforms. | Open-ended scope and distribution obligations. Excludes any unapproved candidate. | **LOCKED** |

Before any phase begins, the owner must explicitly authorize its scope. Final closure requires a green hosted GitHub Actions run on Ubuntu, macOS, and Windows for the exact delivered commit. Local completion of one phase does not unlock another. Material financial and architectural choices require separate decisions under `AGENTS.md`. Detailed plans for Phases 2–7 remain plans only.
